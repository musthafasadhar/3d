## Why this keeps people watching (not generic AI copy)
- **Story first, never a feature list.** Every feature appears only as the answer to a problem on screen:
  the lost report leads to Reports, the privacy fear leads to the access log, the hospital question leads to front desk tools.
- **Hook in under 2 s** with a specific, relatable moment ("4,000 photos") instead of "Introducing…".
- **Something changes every 2–3 s:** a cut, an insert, a new character, or a token-board sting.
- **Two open questions:** "Will he find the report?" (closed at 15 s) and "Is my data safe?" (closed at 28 s).
- **Honest twist at 36 s:** we never claim appointments work (it's "planned" on the site). Being self-aware builds trust and is funnier.
- **Two comment triggers** ("worst hospital experience" and the "AVNI" keyword) push the algorithm, plus a loop ending for rewatches.
