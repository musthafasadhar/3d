### Characters
- **Host:** a Minecraft-like voxel man. Cube head with a separate hair block, a beard block,
  white square eyes with black pupils and a white glint, thick brow blocks, and a box mouth that
  opens in steps. Black tee with a yellow square logo, dark jeans, and white blocky sneakers.
- **Buddy ("Claude"):** a terracotta/coral cuboid (#d97757-ish, Claude's brand colour) with stubby
  block legs and block arms. Eyes are black bars or "^ ^" happy carets. It speaks through arm
  waves and eye changes, and has no mouth.

### Animation
- More energetic (mean motion 1-3, spikes over 20 on the voxel build and confetti).
- Lip-sync is **amplitude-driven** (shown on screen as the "MOUTH" bar track from the voice file).
- Constant idle sway, a hand wave on the CTA, eyes swapping between bars and carets,
  and a squash/hop on the buddy.
- Camera: alternating medium two-shot and CU with dolly moves inside one long take
  (14.7 s, 17.7-32.4 s) and a few hard cuts.
- Showpiece: the scene builds itself from flying voxels (step 3), and a live material
  change (the shirt turns yellow).

### Look
- Deep royal-blue gradient void, a faint perspective grid floor, and soft multicolour bokeh dots
  drifting. Simple 3-point lighting with soft shadows, no DOF blur on the characters.
- UI cards: dark navy rounded panels with subtle borders. Headline chip at the top:
  "STEP N · **TITLE**" with the second half in yellow, plus a 5-dot progress bar.
- Captions: TikTok-style **word-by-word** pop, bold rounded sans (Poppins-like) with a shadow,
  centred on the characters, and keywords in yellow (#FFD21F-ish).

### How it is made (stated by the reel itself)
1. Write a short two-person script.
2. Turn it into two voices (ElevenLabs).
3. Claude writes code (Three.js-style `voxelGuy({hair:"black", beard:true})`, `blockBuddy({color:"#d97757"})`,
   `scene.add(...)`, `camera.cut(...)`) that builds the 3D world. It is **not** AI-generated video.
4. Auto-sync: words, mouth and captions are derived from the voice file.
5. A deterministic frame-by-frame local render: 1,302 frames at 1080x1920, 30 fps.
6. Changes are made by prompting Claude, not by hand-editing.

## Takeaways for our reels
- **Format:** 1080x1920, 30 fps, 35-45 s, a shot every 2-3 s, a hook in the first 2 s, CTA or loop at the end.
- **Cast:** one human host plus one cute mascot buddy with no mouth (cheap to animate, very expressive).
  Design our **own** characters for each topic. Keep the proportions and style, not their designs.
- **Two style tracks we can produce in code:**
  - *Soft low-poly* (Reel A): faceted spheres, warm night-city window, DOF bokeh, pill captions.
  - *Voxel* (Reel B): box primitives, blue void with grid, word-pop captions, UI explainer cards.
- **Animation budget:** idle breathing, sway and blinks, amplitude lip-sync, eye-swap emotions,
  and a slow camera push. This is enough; the dialogue and timing carry the reel.
- Pipeline: see `.claude/skills/3d-character-reel/SKILL.md`.
