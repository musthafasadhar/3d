## Editing tips
- **Make hard cuts in and out.** Each clip starts with a short glitch-in from black, which hides the cut.
- **Add a whoosh or "impact" sound on the red hits.** These happen when a stamp lands, the bubble pops, the pipeline breaks, or the screen glitches.
- **Trim to the voice.** Each clip holds its final frame for about 1 s, so you can cut it shorter or freeze the last frame to cover a longer line.
- **Use the gaps for your screenshots.** Between clips, your Google screenshots or proof elements over your face cam work well. The b-roll covers the stretches where you're explaining.

## Changing text or numbers
All text is in `scenes.js`, one block per clip. For example, the deal amounts in clip 16 are in `CIRCLE_DEALS`. After editing, re-render with:

```
node render.js 16        # one clip
node render.js           # all clips
node render.js --stills  # quick preview frames in out/stills
```
